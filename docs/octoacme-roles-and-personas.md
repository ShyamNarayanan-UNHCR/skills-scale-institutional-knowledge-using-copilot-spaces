# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Security & Compliance Lead

### Role Summary
Security & Compliance Leads protect the organization from security risk, ensure regulatory and policy requirements are considered, and help the team make secure decisions throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Review project work for security requirements and compliance considerations
- Support threat modeling, data privacy checks, and secure design reviews
- Coordinate escalation for security incidents or sensitive vulnerabilities
- Help define risk mitigation plans and approval gates for high-risk work
- Validate that release readiness includes required security controls and sign-offs

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk
- Embed secure-by-design practices into project delivery
- Ensure teams can move fast without creating unacceptable risk

### Typical Communication
- Security review checkpoints and risk discussions
- Policy and compliance check-ins with PMs and engineering leads
- Incident escalation and remediation updates

### Interactions with existing roles
- Works with Developers to review architecture, dependencies, and secure coding practices
- Partners with Project Managers to incorporate security milestones, approvals, and risk tracking into plans
- Supports Product Managers by aligning security constraints with business priorities and release timing
- Coordinates with Stakeholders when security or compliance impacts scope, schedule, or deployment decisions

---

## Quality & Release Manager

### Role Summary
Quality & Release Managers ensure that work is ready for deployment, quality expectations are clear, and the release process is predictable, observable, and low-risk.

### Responsibilities
- Define release readiness criteria and quality gates
- Coordinate QA signoff, regression coverage, and acceptance validation
- Support release planning, rollout sequencing, and rollback decisions
- Monitor operational readiness and post-release verification
- Capture lessons learned from releases and share follow-up actions

### Goals
- Deliver reliable releases with lower operational risk
- Improve confidence in product quality and release process consistency
- Reduce surprise issues during deployment and early adoption

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and QA checklists
- Deployment planning with PMs and engineering teams
- Post-release follow-up with support and stakeholder groups

### Interactions with existing roles
- Works with Developers to confirm that testing, monitoring, and release requirements are met
- Collaborates with Project Managers on release windows, dependencies, and communication plans
- Partners with Product Managers to align quality expectations with business outcomes and customer impact
- Supports Stakeholders by summarizing release health, known risks, and deployment status

---

## Business Analyst / Stakeholder Champion

### Role Summary
Business Analysts and Stakeholder Champions translate business goals, stakeholder needs, and operational realities into clear requirements and actionable work for delivery teams.

### Responsibilities
- Capture business context, pain points, and desired outcomes
- Clarify scope, assumptions, and decision drivers for backlog items
- Validate requirements against stakeholder priorities and constraints
- Help connect customer or business feedback to product and delivery decisions
- Support prioritization by explaining trade-offs and impact

### Goals
- Improve requirement clarity and business alignment
- Reduce ambiguity between stakeholders and delivery teams
- Ensure work remains connected to customer and organizational value

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder interviews, workshops, and prioritization discussions
- Requirements refinement with Product and delivery teams
- Status updates and decision summaries for leadership stakeholders

### Interactions with existing roles
- Works closely with Product Managers to refine problem statements and success metrics
- Supports Project Managers by clarifying dependencies, scope assumptions, and stakeholder expectations
- Helps Developers understand business intent and acceptance criteria behind requested changes
- Provides context to Stakeholders on trade-offs, risks, and expected outcomes

---

## Customer Success / Support Liaison

### Role Summary
Customer Success and Support Liaisons connect delivery teams with real-world customer experience, operational issues, and post-release feedback so improvements are prioritized effectively.

### Responsibilities
- Gather feedback from customers, support teams, and usage data
- Identify recurring issues, adoption barriers, and service pain points
- Escalate high-impact customer concerns to the appropriate owners
- Help prioritize fixes and follow-up improvements based on customer impact
- Share release or product updates with support and customer-facing teams

### Goals
- Improve customer satisfaction and adoption
- Turn operational feedback into actionable delivery priorities
- Reduce friction between support cycles and product improvement work

### Typical Communication
- Support handoff and escalation channels
- Customer feedback reviews and issue triage discussions
- Release communication and follow-up planning

### Interactions with existing roles
- Provides Product Managers with customer insight that informs roadmap decisions
- Supports Project Managers in tracking external risks, dependencies, and communication needs
- Shares issues and usage patterns with Developers to guide fixes and improvements
- Keeps Stakeholders informed on customer impact and expected mitigation steps

---

## Platform / Infrastructure Partner

### Role Summary
Platform and Infrastructure Partners provide the technical foundations needed to build, deploy, and operate solutions reliably, including environments, automation, and observability.

### Responsibilities
- Provision and maintain environments, pipelines, and deployment tooling
- Support automation for builds, tests, deployments, and monitoring
- Help improve reliability, observability, and operational readiness
- Coordinate environment access and infrastructure dependencies with delivery teams
- Support recovery planning and technical troubleshooting during incidents

### Goals
- Improve delivery speed without compromising reliability
- Reduce environment-related blockers and deployment friction
- Enable engineering teams to operate effectively at scale

### Typical Communication
- Platform or infrastructure planning meetings
- Deployment readiness check-ins and environment reviews
- Incident response and operational status coordination

### Interactions with existing roles
- Works with Developers to enable deployment pipelines, tooling, and environment health
- Collaborates with Project Managers on release dependencies, risk planning, and rollout timing
- Supports Product Managers by helping assess feasibility and operational trade-offs
- Coordinates with Stakeholders when infrastructure constraints impact timeline or service reliability

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These roles help teams recognize that product delivery depends on both strategic ownership and operational execution across engineering, governance, support, and customer-facing functions.
